## Headline

**Traffic volume is small.** Top page is the homepage at 23 clicks / 1,191 impressions over 16 months. No URL crosses the 100-clicks/month threshold flagged as "CRITICAL" in the brief — so there are **zero CRITICAL gaps** by that definition. The site is low-traffic; the SEO risk is reputational/long-tail, not raw click loss.

**The bigger finding is destination drift, not redirect absence.** 45 redirect rules exist in `next.config.ts`. They mostly point at the right shape (`/services/<slug>`, `/solutions/<slug>`, `/stream/<slug>`). But **12 of the 45 destinations are MDX files that don't yet exist** in `content/`. After cutover, those redirects will 301 → 404. That is the actual silent-bleed risk.

**Three URLs have zero redirect coverage** and earned non-trivial impressions: `/java`, `/seo-for-entrepreneurs`, and `/java-seo-technical-audits-coffee-shops` (986 impressions, 45 GA4 sessions — this one IS redirected; calling it out as the top organic landing page that needs special attention).

Renaming the MDX is cleaner because the redirect URLs are public-facing SEO commitments and the slug structure should be considered when content is built.
