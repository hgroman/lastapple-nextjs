## What Most Agencies Actually Do

A typical small-business marketing engagement looks like this: a junior account manager runs Yoast or Semrush, exports a PDF, and emails it once a month. Someone in a Slack channel writes a 500-word blog post that nobody reads. A paid-search specialist tweaks Google Ads bids on a Friday afternoon. You pay $2,500/month for the bundle.

The reports look professional. The needle doesn't move.

## What We Do Differently

The work is the same — technical SEO, content, paid search, analytics. The leverage is different. AI agents do the repeatable parts under human editorial control. The operator (Hank) makes the calls that require taste, context, and accountability.

### AI-Assisted Technical SEO Audits

We run audits against `wp-cli`, the Google Search Console API, and PageSpeed Insights — not just a third-party crawler. Every finding is checked against real query data, not hypothetical keywords. When we flag a Core Web Vitals issue, we've already opened the file that needs to change.

Reports are markdown, version-controlled, and shared in the project workspace. No 60-page PDFs that get filed and forgotten.

### Content Generation Pipelines

We use LLMs to draft content. We do not publish LLM drafts unedited. The pipeline is:

1. Topic research against your GSC query data (what people actually search to find you)
2. Outline generated and reviewed by the operator
3. Draft generated against your brand voice (we maintain a voice spec for each client)
4. Human editorial pass — fact-check, voice match, link strategy
5. Schema markup, internal links, image alt text
6. Publish, then ping the Google Indexing API for immediate re-crawl

The output is content that ranks because it's actually useful, not because it's stuffed with keywords. The volume is sustainable because the operator isn't writing every word from scratch.

### Automated GA4 and GSC Reporting

Most agency reports are "what happened." Useful reports tell you "what changed and what to do about it." We run automated weekly pulls from GA4 and GSC into a dashboard you can read in five minutes. When traffic shifts, we know within a day, not a month.

UTM hygiene is enforced. Campaign attribution is auditable. If you ran a $400 LinkedIn ad and got three sessions, you'll know.

### Paid Search Bid Management

We use Google Ads' built-in ML (Target CPA, Maximize Conversions) plus a manual review cadence — not "set it and forget it." Bids get checked weekly against conversion data. Negative keyword lists get pruned monthly. Budget gets reallocated when a campaign underperforms instead of running for a quarter on inertia.

We don't take a percentage of ad spend. Flat fee. You don't pay us more when your budget goes up.

### Indexing API and SERP Hygiene

When we publish or update a page, we submit it to Google's Indexing API the same hour. We track which URLs are indexed, which are excluded, and why. Schema markup is validated against the Rich Results Test. This is plumbing work that most agencies skip — and it's the difference between a content investment that pays off in days versus weeks.
