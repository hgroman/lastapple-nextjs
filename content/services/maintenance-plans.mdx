## Last Apple Maintenance Plans: A Tiered Approach with AI Integration

Choose the plan that fits where your site is today. Each tier builds on the one before it, so you can start with essential upkeep and grow into performance, SEO, custom development, and AI as your needs do.

## The Plans

### WordPress Essentials — $149/month

Designed as a reliable foundation, this plan ensures your WordPress site operates smoothly with essential maintenance services. Ideal for businesses focused on core upkeep, the WordPress Essentials plan includes:

- **Core WordPress Updates** — keeps your site running on the latest stable version of WordPress to maximize security and performance.
- **Regular Plugin Updates** — updates all installed plugins to support functionality and enhance site security.
- **Theme Compatibility Updates** — updates your theme to align with WordPress improvements and security standards.
- **Daily Data Backups** — safeguards your website content with daily backups to protect against potential data loss.
- **Security Monitoring** — monitors your website to help identify and address any security concerns.

### Growth Accelerator — $249/month *(Most Popular)*

Building on the foundation of the WordPress Essentials plan, the Growth Accelerator level introduces enhanced security, performance, and SEO features for businesses looking to grow. **All features from the Essentials plan, plus:**

- **Performance Optimization** — regularly fine-tunes your site for faster load times and improved overall efficiency.
- **Uptime Monitoring** — notifies you promptly if your website goes offline, minimizing potential downtime.
- **Monthly Maintenance Reports** — provides clear, monthly summaries of updates, backups, and website health metrics.
- **Priority Support for Critical Issues** — delivers swift response to urgent issues, helping prevent prolonged disruptions.

### Business Transformer — $499/month

The Business Transformer plan amplifies your website's capabilities with advanced tools designed to support growth, SEO, and custom development. **All features from the Growth Accelerator plan, plus:**

- **Advanced SEO Implementations** — boosts your site's search engine presence with comprehensive SEO enhancements.
- **AI-Enhanced Blog Posts (Limited)** — leverages AI insights to help you create engaging, optimized content for your audience.
- **Basic CRM Integration** — connects your website with customer relationship management tools for streamlined client engagement.
- **Expanded Content Updates** — provides more extensive monthly content updates to keep your website fresh and relevant.
- **Custom WordPress Development** — enables tailored website functionality to meet unique business requirements.
- **Unlimited On-Demand Development Tasks** — offers flexibility for ongoing improvements and customization as needed.

### Enterprise AI Solutions — $999/month

The Enterprise AI Solutions plan represents the pinnacle of website growth and optimization, integrating powerful AI-driven tools to maximize performance, SEO, and user engagement. **Everything from the Business Transformer plan, plus:**

- **AI-Powered Conversion Optimization** — applies AI insights to refine user experience and boost on-site conversions.
- **AI SEO Powerhouse** — continuously optimizes your site's SEO through advanced AI tools, helping you stay ahead of the competition.
- **AI-Powered Content Generation Engine** — uses AI to create high-quality, relevant content, keeping your site fresh and engaging.
- **Advanced CRM Integration and Workflow Automation** — enables seamless CRM connectivity and automates workflows for streamlined customer engagement.
- **Unlimited Development Tasks on Demand** — provides unrestricted development support, allowing for constant customization and refinement.
