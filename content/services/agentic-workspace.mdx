## What This Is

Every business owner has now asked some version of *"how should we be using AI?"* — and most of them are asking for this without having the words for it.

Not a chat account. Not a subscription with a clever prompt saved in it. **A workspace that belongs to one named person, knows their business, remembers, and is allowed to see exactly as much as it should.**

We have built one and handed it over. End to end, it took nine hours.

## The Part Nobody Else Is Selling

Provisioning an assistant is easy and getting easier every month. That is not the hard part and it is not what should worry you.

The hard part is the question underneath it: **what is this thing allowed to see?**

Before the first workspace we built was permitted to start, we ran an exposure audit across everything it could have reached — **431 tasks, 81 initiatives and 42 documents** — and made a decision, record class by record class, about what belonged inside its horizon and what did not. That happened *before* boot, not after an incident.

This is the whole difference between a demo and something you can hand to a client. An assistant that can read is a demo. An assistant that can read *your client's data* is a liability until somebody has decided, in writing, which parts. Most people discover they never made that decision at the moment it stops being hypothetical.

**We do the deciding first, and we write it down**, because the answer has to survive the person who configured it.

## What Actually Gets Built

- **Its own machine.** Dedicated infrastructure, not a tenant in something shared. The isolation is structural rather than a setting.
- **Its own identity and memory.** It is a persistent thing with a name, not a session that forgets you. Work handed to it on Tuesday is still there on Friday.
- **Mailbox and access wiring.** It can receive, read and act inside the boundary it has been given.
- **A structured intake.** This is where the grounding comes from — the business, the clients, the work in flight, the vocabulary. A generic assistant asked to help with your business will confidently invent your business. This one is told.
- **The exposure audit**, as above, before anything boots.
- **A live demonstration** against real systems at handover, because a workspace that only works in a screenshot is not a workspace.

## Why It Is Nine Hours And Not Nine Weeks

Because the pattern already exists and has been run. The first one was expensive to figure out; the second one inherits the intake structure, the audit method and the wiring.

We will tell you plainly which of those hours are yours and which are ours: the audit is the part that cannot be shortcut, because it depends on your data and nobody else's.

## Who This Isn't For

**Not for you if you want it to see everything.** The exposure audit is the product. If the requirement is "give it access to all of it and we'll sort out the boundaries later," we are the wrong shop — that is the engagement we would decline, and this page is where we say so before you spend a call finding out.

**Not for you if nobody will use it.** This is built for one named person who has actual work to hand it. Bought for a team in general, it becomes a login nobody opens.

**Not for you if you want a chatbot on your website.** Different product, genuinely — that is [AI chatbot work](/solutions/ai-chatbot-solutions) and it is cheaper. Buy that if that is the need.

**Not for you if the answer has to be a subscription.** This is a build with an owner, not a seat you rent. If the budget shape is per-user-per-month, the shape of this is wrong for you.
