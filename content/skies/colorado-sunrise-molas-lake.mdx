Molas Pass sits at 10,900 feet between Silverton and Durango. On 6 July 2025 the drone flew the corridor south from Silverton in eight flights between 5:55 and 6:32 in the morning, following the light down toward the lake.

The map next to this film is the one worth looking at closely. The sunrise cluster north of Durango — the warm dots against desert ground — is not a decoration. It is this flight. Those are the actual coordinates the footage came from, logged as it was shot, which is why the film and the map can sit next to each other and mean something.

It went up on 8 July, two days later, while the trip was still running. It ended that same day.
