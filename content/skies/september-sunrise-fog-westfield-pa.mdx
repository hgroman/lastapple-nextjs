The drone went up at 7:06 in the morning with the sun four degrees above the horizon, and the fog was still lying in the valley. That is the whole film: cold air on the Cowanesque, a town waking up under it, and a string arrangement written to the footage afterward.

It was published the same day it was shot.

That is not a boast about speed, it is what the working method actually is. The flight, the edit and the score happened between dawn and evening on 7 September 2025, on the road, in the middle of a cross-country trip. Nothing was storyboarded. The music exists because the fog did.

The map beside this film is the trip that produced it — every flight logged with its own coordinates, so you can see where this morning sits inside the larger route.
