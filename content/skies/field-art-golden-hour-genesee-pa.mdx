Contour farming is a soil-conservation technique. You plough along the curve of a hill instead of down it, so the rain has nowhere to run. From a thousand feet up it stops being agronomy and turns into drawing — bands of standing corn against tilled earth, following the shape of the ground.

Nobody designed that to be looked at. It is the longest film in the set at just under ten minutes, because the piano needed room and the shapes kept going.

Shot on the evening of 5 September 2025 and published the following night — a one-day turnaround, which is roughly how long it takes to write to something you have just seen.
