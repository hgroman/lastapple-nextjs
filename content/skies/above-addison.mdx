A film decides where you look. This does not.

It is a single equirectangular capture stitched from a full rotation over the Canisteo valley, served as an interactive sphere — drag it and you are standing where the drone was standing, looking wherever you want. Cornfields, treelines, and whatever the weather was doing that afternoon.

Included here because it answers a question the films cannot: not what it looked like, but what it was like to be up there.
